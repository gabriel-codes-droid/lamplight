Lamplight

A shadow-puppet story you play with your bare hands. Sit down and let the lamp do the rest.

Get ready

  • 1Turn on Hand TrackingIn your Quest settings
  • 2Put your controllers downThe game uses only your hands
  • 3Enter VR and hold up both handsThe story starts when both are seen

Shapes you will make

BirdTwo hands, wings out
DogFlat snout, ears up
RabbitTwo fingers up

No headset? Press Space to step through the story.
