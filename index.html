Lamplight

A shadow-puppet story you play with your bare hands. Sit down and let the lamp do the rest.

Get ready

  1. Turn on Hand Tracking in your Quest settings.
  2. Put your controllers down.
  3. Enter VR and hold up both hands.

Shapes you will make

Bird
Two hands, wings out
Dog
Thumb up, mouth open
Rabbit
Two fingers up

No headset? Press Space to step through the story.
